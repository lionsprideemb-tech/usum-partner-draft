USUM Bank Hunt Checklist

Use this while searching Pokémon Bank. Any member of the listed evolution line counts unless the row says the exact Alolan form matters. The prep recommendation is built for a fun Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon adventure—not competitive perfection. Good-enough natures and abilities are encouraged; the goal is to make every pick feel awesome to use.

Games we can use: Pokémon X, Pokémon Y, Omega Ruby / Alpha Sapphire, and Ultra Sun / Ultra Moon. The catch boxes below now prioritize the easiest route from those games.
3DS transfer note: Keep anything needed for this run in the Gen 6/7 + Pokémon Bank ecosystem; do not send it into HOME first because it cannot return to Bank/USUM.
Hidden Ability tip: In USUM, an SOS ally can start having its Hidden Ability after a chain of 10. The HA chance is 5% at 10–19, 10% at 20–29, and 15% from 30 onward. At 30+, called Pokémon also have 4 guaranteed perfect IVs. Use an Adrenaline Orb and keep one caller alive. Transfer-only HAs must come from an older game / existing HA parent; Gen 7 has no Ability Patch.
